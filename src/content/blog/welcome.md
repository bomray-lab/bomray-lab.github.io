Welcome to **Bomray Lab**.

We build lightweight productivity apps that help teams simplify repetitive administrative work.

Our first product, **Needs Attention for Jira**, helps Jira Cloud administrators review projects, custom fields, filters, and dashboards that may require attention — with clear evidence and without automatic cleanup.

## What you'll find here

The Bomray Lab resource center includes:

- Product updates
- Technical documentation
- Jira administration guides
- Atlassian Forge development notes
- Best practices and troubleshooting articles
